NAVLE Mindset Check

Your Inner Critic and your Inner Coach

Preparing for high-stakes exams like the NAVLE can often expose the stories we tell ourselves quietly. Some of that inner dialogue can be helpful. Think of this as your Inner Coach. Some of it, though, subtly works against you. This is your Inner Critic. This Mindset Check tool will help you see which voice is louder during NAVLE preparation, and which helpful habits you already have.

There are no right or wrong answers. Your responses stay on your device and are not sent to anyone.
